Our Story

How a small charcoal grill in Lahore grew into a restaurant families keep coming back to.

Since 2009

Cooked with care, since day one.

FLAMES started in 2009 with one small charcoal grill and a simple idea. Cook everything fresh, treat every guest well, and never take shortcuts. That idea has not changed, even as the restaurant has grown.

Today we serve three kinds of food under one roof. Desi food that reminds you of home, Chinese food that hits the spot, and fast food that is quick without cutting corners. Our chefs train for months before they join the kitchen, and our staff are trained to treat every guest like a guest in their own home.

We are proud to be a part of Lahore's food scene, and we are proud that so many families have made FLAMES a part of their own celebrations, big and small.
